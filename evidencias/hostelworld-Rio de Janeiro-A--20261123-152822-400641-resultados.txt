The chain of El Misti Hostels offers the safest stay in Rio de Janeiro. Misti Ipanema is just 1 minute walk away from Ipanema Beach, where your safety is also assured as we have a partnership with a beach point called Jota, number 86. There, you have free wifi and can leave your things in the beach tent. Copacabana Beach is a 10-minute walk away. In addition, it's located just 1 minute walk away from the Nossa Senhora de Paz metro station. It is the most recent addition to the El Misti Hostels, designed to match the high level of the Ipanema neighborhood. The Misti Ipanema was designed to meet all the needs of travellers while maintaining a hostel atmosphere.

El Misti Ipanema has mixed dormitories for 4, 6, 8 and 12 people with private bathrooms. All equipped with air conditioning, individual wardrobes and bedding.

It also has private double, twin, triple and quadruple rooms with private bathrooms. All equipped with air conditioning and bedding.

The Ipanema neighborhood is one of the most famous in the country and worldwide. It has a heritage of artists inspired by its beauty and its bohemian vibe. Today is the peak of tourism in Rio. People from all over the world come to visit their traditional bars, listen to live music and watch their postcard sunset. El Misti Ipanema is located at number 9, the young point of the neighborhood and an unbeatable location.

A relaxed atmosphere and a 24-hour multi-lingual staff willing to give you some advice on excursions and tours, is what you will find in this hostel. Here you can meet other travelers like you and have an unforgettable stay!

Summary of traveller reviews

El Misti Hostel Ipanema in Rio de Janeiro is praised for its fantastic location, just a block from Ipanema beach and close to the metro. Guests consistently highlight the incredibly friendly and helpful staff, with many mentioning specific individuals like Cristal, Eric, and Rodrigo for their exceptional service. The hostel is highly social, offering numerous activities that make it easy to meet people, making it ideal for solo travellers and those seeking a lively atmosphere. Some recent reviews note that rooms can be small and cramped, and occasionally mention issues with shower functionality or cleanliness in bathrooms, though the overall sentiment remains overwhelmingly positive.
