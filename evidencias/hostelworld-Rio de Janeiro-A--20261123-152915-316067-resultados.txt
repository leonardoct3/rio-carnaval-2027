About


Welcome to Copacabana Central Hostel – a social and budget-friendly hostel in the heart of Copacabana.

We are located:

📍 3 minutes from Siqueira Campos Metro Station
🏖️ 10 minutes walking from Copacabana Beach – Posto 3
🎉 Directly across from Substation Club
🍛 Close to supermarkets, bakeries, bars and many cheap local food options

We are a great option for travelers looking for an affordable stay in a central location, close to everything.

*Rio Roots*
Our part of Copacabana is not only made for tourists. You will also find local restaurants, bakeries, bars and everyday Rio life around us.

It is a great place to experience a more authentic side of Copacabana while still being close to the beach, metro and nightlife.

Why stay with us?

✔️ Great budget option in Copacabana
✔️ Friendly and social atmosphere
✔️ International team always ready to help
✔️ Personalised tips about Rio
✔️ Tips on how to enjoy Rio and keep your phone and belongings safe
✔️ Cheap food options nearby
✔️ Board games and common areas
✔️ Metro just around the corner
✔️ Easy access to the property via door code

Our team is happy to help you with recommendations about beaches, nightlife, samba, local food, transport and things to do in Rio.
