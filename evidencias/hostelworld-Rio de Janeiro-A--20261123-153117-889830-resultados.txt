About


Hostel New Ipanema is located in the heart of Ipanema, one of the safest and most desirable neighborhoods in the city. We are just three blocks from the beach, three streets from Lagoa Rodrigo de Freitas, and one block from the metro, making it easy to get around Rio. Our space is cozy and perfect for travelers from all over the world seeking an authentic and fun experience. The atmosphere is friendly, informal, and full of positive energy, with a dedicated team committed to making your stay unforgettable.

Close to restaurants, bars, and shops, we are the ideal meeting point to explore Rio de Janeiro. Enjoy our services such as luggage storage, early check-in, and Day Use after check-out. If you arrive before 8 am, please let us know, as our reception opens at that time.

We have the best tips about Rio to help you make the most of your trip and feel like a local during your stay.

Policies: Check-in from 1 pm, check-out until 12 pm. Cancellation 14 days in advance. Payment upon arrival via cash or PIX (available for Brazilians, Argentinians, and Chileans).

Special event: Reservations made between April 30th and May 4th, 2025, are subject to a non-refundable cancellation policy and require mandatory prepayment. Failure to complete the payment may result in the cancellation of the booking. A minimum stay of 3 nights is required.

Important! To guarantee your reservation, you need to contact the property and share your card's CVV code. Not providing this code may result in the cancellation of your reservation. Remember, you will only be charged in case of cancellation.

If you'd like any adjustments or further assistance, just let me know!
