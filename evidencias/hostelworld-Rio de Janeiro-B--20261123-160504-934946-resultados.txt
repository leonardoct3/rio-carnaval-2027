About


Our location couldn't be better! We are in Ipanema, in the South Zone of the marvelous city, where you can see the most applauded sunset in Brazil up close.

Regarding our rooms we have: Private Suites - Double rooms with single or double beds and a large private balcony. TV, minibar, AC, private bathroom, and a beautiful view, breakfast included. Shared Rooms - Single beds and bunk beds only, for more comfort and less tightness. Bathrooms inside in all rooms. Light, plugs, and individual lockers for each bed. Regular breakfast price: 44.90 BRL per day. If you book it in advance for your entire stay, it costs 34.90 BRL per day.

We offer some free activities during the week
There is one activity per day, including trails (Morro dos Irmãos trail, Pedra Bonita trail, Morro da Urca trail - Pão de Açucar), karaoke night, tattoo night, yacht party (price to be paid separately) and free caipirinhas every night from 8:00 pm to 8:30 pm.

More Ipanema Policy and Conditions:

Cancellation Policy:
* Cancellations can be made free of charge up to 14 days before the check-in date. In case of late cancellation or No-show the amount charged is non refundable.
* Reservations for the New Year and Carnival periods are non-refundable. Free cancellation is available up to 30 days before the check-in date. In case of late cancellation or No-show the amount charged is non refundable.

Payment Policy:
* 50% of the total stay amount will be charged 14 days before check-in, using the card registered on the platform. If payment is not received, the reservation will be canceled.
* For reservations made during the New Year or Carnival periods, the full amount of the reservation will be charged 30 days before check-in. If payment is not received, the reservation will be canceled.
