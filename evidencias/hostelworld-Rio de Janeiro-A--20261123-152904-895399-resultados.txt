About


Ipanema Beach Hostel is set in the Ipanema district in Rio de Janeiro. Our location couldn't be better! We are located at the beach block in the best point of Ipanema beach, one block from the subway and mall, surrounded by restaurants, bars and clubs. Southern area of the wonderful city and a block from the beach that inspired one of the most famous songs in the world and where you will be able to see closely the most applauded sunset in Brazil. Ipanema Beach Hostel features free Wi-Fi throughout the property, a flat-screen TV is available. You will find a 24-hour front desk at the property. The staff is dedicated to ensuring that you enjoy the highest levels of services and greatest experience in Rio de Janeiro. All the time through our services of shared dorms, suites, living areas and tours, we have hospitality as the core of our work. In order for you to leave Rio a little bit more carioca (person from Rio).

Our location couldn't be better! We are located in Ipanema, in the southern part of the wonderful city and one block from the beach that inspired one of the most famous songs in the world and where you can see the most applauded sunset in Brazil up close.

Ipanema Beach Hostel offers clean, stylish and comfortable dorms and private rooms.
The shared rooms have built-in bunk beds only, with lighting and individual light, power plug and locker, as well as separate bathrooms/showers inside all dorms.
In the suites, guests will enjoy our room service, balcony, perfect for relaxing and enjoying the view, 32-inch TV, cable programming, lockers, new air conditioning, mini bar and showers stall.
Finally, everyone can count on a high-speed Wi-Fi, air conditioning throughout the building.
