About


Immerse yourself in the vibrant heart of Rio at Leblon Beach Hostel!

Imagine waking up just steps away from the city's most charming beach, with the carioca sun calling you for an unforgettable day.

Our hostel is the perfect meeting point for travellers seeking a modern and welcoming atmosphere.

Relax in our common areas, designed to socialise and create incredible memories. Share travel stories with other guests, exchange tips on the best spots in Rio, and prepare to explore all that the wonderful city has to offer.

Located on General San Martin, 360, in Leblon, you'll be surrounded by trendy restaurants, bars with live music, and exclusive shops. And of course, Leblon beach, with its natural beauty and contagious energy, will always be at your disposal.

Come and experience Rio de Janeiro in a unique and authentic way at Leblon Beach Hostel!

Your carioca paradise awaits you.
